## Working notes

- **Adding/renaming guides:** edit `metadata.json`, then run `python3 scripts/validate_metadata.py` and `python3 scripts/build.py`; commit the regenerated articles, `sitemap.xml` and `feed.xml`. CI fails if you forget.
- **Removing a guide:** delete the file, remove its entry, add `old-slug → successor-slug` to `site.redirects`.
- **Changing the article chrome** (breadcrumbs, related guides, staleness copy): edit the templates in `scripts/build.py`, re-run it — the injection is idempotent and replaces the previous `<!-- eadr:* -->` blocks in all 101 files.
- **Staleness rule:** 365 days from `verified`, evaluated at build time (`data-built`) and again in the browser. Change `STALE_AFTER_DAYS` in `scripts/build.py` and `index.html` together.
