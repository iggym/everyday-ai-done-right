Read Physical Labels & Mail Aloud Using Built-In AI

Point and Speak uses your iPhone's Neural Engine to read physical text aloud exactly where you point your finger—completely offline, free forever, and built right into iOS.

Replaces dedicated OCR reading machines (e.g., OrCam Read)

$4,500 $0

The Walkthrough

Launch Instantly

Open the Magnifier app on your iPhone. To make this instant from any screen, set it to your Accessibility Shortcut:

Settings > Accessibility > Accessibility Shortcut > Select 'Magnifier'

Enable Detection

Tap the "Detection" icon (a square with dots) at the bottom center of the screen.

Select Point and Speak

Choose "Point and Speak" from the detection menu that appears.

Position the Camera

Hold your phone about 12 inches away from the object (a medicine bottle, a microwave keypad, or a mail label), keeping it parallel to the text.

Point and Listen

Move your finger across the screen. The iPhone will read aloud the exact text located under (or above) your finger in real time, using spatial AI to map the physical world.

⚠️ What Breaks

This tool requires an iPhone running iOS 17 or later. For the precise spatial "pointing" feature, a Pro model with a LiDAR scanner is highly recommended; non-Pro models fall back to standard 2D on-screen text detection, which is less precise for dense keypads. It strictly reads text—it will not describe complex visual scenes (e.g., "a person wearing a red shirt"). Poor lighting or highly glossy surfaces (like some pill bottles) can also trip up the local OCR.

Three Things to Know

The Accessibility Shortcut

Triple-clicking the side button bypasses the home screen entirely, launching Magnifier instantly—a crucial time-saver and privacy win for blind users.

The LiDAR Advantage

On Pro iPhones, the tool uses depth mapping to understand exactly which physical button is *above* your finger, not just what text is anywhere on the camera feed.

The Offline Reality

Because the OCR and text-to-speech run entirely on the local Neural Engine, it requires zero cell service or Wi-Fi to function. It works identically in an airplane or a basement.
