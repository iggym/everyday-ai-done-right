Instantly remove backgrounds from any photo for your creative projects using the subject isolation feature built into iOS 16+ and Android 14+—free forever, no card, and it runs entirely on your device with no uploads required.

The Walkthrough

Step 1 of 5: Open Native Gallery

Open your device's native Photos (iOS) or Gallery (Android) app. Do not open a third-party editing app.

Step 2 of 5: Locate the Image

Find the image containing the subject you want to isolate (e.g., a person, pet, or distinct object).

Step 3 of 5: Long-Press the Subject

Press and hold your finger directly on the subject. You will see a subtle glow or shimmer animate around the edges, indicating the OS has isolated the layer.

Step 4 of 5: Copy or Share

A context menu will appear. Tap "Copy" to place it on your clipboard, or "Share" to send it directly to another app.

Step 5 of 5: Paste Into Your Project

Paste the cutout directly into your notes, a messaging app, or a free design tool like Photopea.

⚠️ What Breaks

This feature requires iOS 16 or later, or Android 14 (or One UI 6.1+) or later. Older devices will not have this capability. Extremely busy or low-contrast backgrounds may result in imperfect edges, requiring a secondary touch-up in a free editor. Because it is a local OS feature, you cannot batch-process hundreds of images at once like a dedicated server tool.

Three Things You Might Not Know

1. The "Long-Press" trigger +
The isolation is activated by a simple long-press, not a hidden menu button, making it faster than opening a dedicated app. (Traces to Apple Support documentation)
2. Cross-platform parity +
This is not an Apple-exclusive trick; Android 14+ includes native subject isolation, ensuring the workflow is accessible regardless of your device ecosystem. (Traces to OS built-in family research)
3. Zero data leakage +
Because the Neural Engine processes the image locally, your unpublished creative work never touches a third-party server, avoiding the privacy trade-offs of "free" web-based removers. (Traces to T0 Zero-Cost Integrity verification)
