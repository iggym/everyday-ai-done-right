Step 1: Access the Free Interface

Navigate to claude.ai and sign in with an email address or Google account. You will not be asked for payment information.

Step 2: Gather Your Documentation

Collect your move-out date, the original deposit amount, the landlord's itemized deduction list (if provided), and any relevant photos or communication logs.

Step 3: Apply the Prompt Template

Paste the following template into the chat, replacing the bracketed sections with your specific facts:

Act as a tenant rights advocate. I need to draft a formal demand letter to my former landlord regarding an unjust security deposit withholding. Here are the facts: - Move-out date: [Date] - Original deposit amount: $[Amount] - Amount withheld: $[Amount] - Landlord's stated reason: [e.g., "carpet cleaning", "wall damage"] - My counter-evidence: [e.g., "I have move-in/move-out photos showing no damage", "Normal wear and tear"] - State/Jurisdiction: [Your State] Please draft a formal, firm, but professional demand letter. Requirements: 1. State the facts clearly and chronologically. 2. Cite general principles of tenant rights regarding "normal wear and tear" vs. "damage". 3. Demand the return of the withheld amount within 14 days. 4. Mention that failure to comply will result in filing a claim in small claims court. 5. Format it as a ready-to-print letter with placeholders for my name, address, and the landlord's details.

Step 4: Review, Refine, and Send

Read the generated letter. If it sounds too aggressive or misses a detail, reply with: "Soften the tone in paragraph 2". Once satisfied, print, sign, and send via Certified Mail with a return receipt requested for legal proof of delivery.

What Breaks (Limitations)

Claude's free tier enforces a dynamic limit of roughly 15–40 messages per 5-hour window [[9]]. Heavy iteration will hit this cap. Additionally, AI cannot provide formal legal advice or guarantee compliance with hyper-specific local municipal codes; it provides a strong, generalized template that you must verify against your local tenant union or legal aid resources.

Three Things to Know

1. Rolling 5-Hour Window +
The message limit is not a flat daily quota. It operates on a rolling 5-hour window from your first message. If you hit the limit, you must wait for the oldest messages in that window to age out.
2. Certified Mail is Mandatory +
A demand letter only has legal weight if you can prove the landlord received it. Always use USPS Certified Mail with a return receipt. The postage cost (~$4) is a necessary part of this workflow.
3. Normal Wear and Tear +
Landlords frequently conflate "damage" with "normal wear and tear" (e.g., faded paint, minor carpet traffic). The prompt explicitly instructs the AI to highlight this legal distinction, which is your strongest defense.
