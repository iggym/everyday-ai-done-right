Turn Messy Meeting Notes Into Structured Action Items for Free

Paste messy meeting notes into Groq’s free playground and instantly get a structured table of action items, owners, and a ready-to-send follow-up email. No credit card, no subscription, capped at 14,400 requests per day.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Access the Playground

Navigate to the Groq Cloud Playground and create a free account using only an email address. No credit card is required.

Step 2: Configure the Model

Select the llama-3.3-70b-versatile model from the model dropdown menu to ensure high-quality structured data extraction.

Step 3: Use the Prompt Template

Copy the prompt below, paste it into the chat window, and append your raw notes at the bottom.

You are an expert executive assistant. Analyze the following raw meeting notes. Output exactly three sections:
1. A Markdown table of Action Items with columns: Task, Owner, Deadline.
2. A 3-sentence summary of key decisions made.
3. A polite, professional follow-up email draft to all attendees summarizing the above.

Raw Notes: [PASTE YOUR NOTES HERE]

Step 4: Execute and Export

Click "Run". Copy the structured output directly into your preferred documentation or project management tool.

⚠️ What Breaks (Limitations)

  • The free tier enforces a hard limit of 30 requests per minute and 14,400 requests per day.
  • The context window is finite; very long transcripts (exceeding ~8,000 words) must be split into two separate requests.
  • During peak global usage, Groq may occasionally return a "429 Rate limit exceeded" error. Wait 60 seconds and retry.
  1. Anonymize sensitive data: Remove real names, financial figures, or confidential client details before pasting into any third-party AI tool.
  2. Pacing: The 30 requests/minute limit means you can process about one meeting note every 2 seconds. Batch processing is highly efficient.
  3. Error handling: If you hit a rate limit, the playground will return a 429 error. Do not refresh; simply wait 60 seconds and click Run again.
