⚠️ What Breaks (Limitations)

  • The free tier enforces a hard limit of 30 requests per minute and 14,400 requests per day.
  • The context window is finite; very long transcripts (exceeding ~8,000 words) must be split into two separate requests.
  • During peak global usage, Groq may occasionally return a "429 Rate limit exceeded" error. Wait 60 seconds and retry.
  1. Anonymize sensitive data: Remove real names, financial figures, or confidential client details before pasting into any third-party AI tool.
  2. Pacing: The 30 requests/minute limit means you can process about one meeting note every 2 seconds. Batch processing is highly efficient.
  3. Error handling: If you hit a rate limit, the playground will return a 429 error. Do not refresh; simply wait 60 seconds and click Run again.
