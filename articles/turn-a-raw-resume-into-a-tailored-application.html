Step 1: Open Google AI Studio and Select the Model

Navigate to aistudio.google.com and sign in with any Google account. In the top model selection dropdown, choose Gemini 1.5 Flash or Gemini 2.0 Flash.

Step 2: Feed the Job Description as Context

Before pasting your resume, prime the model with the exact requirements of the target role using this prompt:

You are an expert technical recruiter and resume strategist. I am going to provide a target job description. Read it carefully, extract the core technical requirements, mandatory skills, and cultural keywords, and acknowledge receipt before I provide my resume.

JOB DESCRIPTION:
[PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION HERE]

Step 3: Input Your Raw Resume and Request Alignment

Supply your current resume text along with specific restructuring instructions:

Here is my current resume. Rewrite and tailor the experience bullet points to directly address the keywords and priorities identified in the job description. Do not invent false experience, but reframe my achievements using active voice and quantifiable metrics where applicable. Output the result in clean Markdown format.

CURRENT RESUME:
[PASTE YOUR RESUME TEXT HERE]

Step 4: Review and Export

Review the generated Markdown output for accuracy, then copy and paste the text directly into your preferred document editor for final polish.

⚠️ What Breaks

  • Rate Limit Ceilings: Sending more than 15 requests per minute (RPM) during rapid trial-and-error triggers a temporary rate-limit pause.
  • Daily Quota Cap: The free tier cuts off after 1,500 requests per day (RPD), resetting automatically at midnight UTC.
  • Formatting Stripping: Copying directly from complex PDF layouts introduces broken characters; always supply clean plain text.

Three Things to Know

1. Context Anchoring +
Feeding the job description before your resume prevents the model from hallucinating irrelevant skills or drifting from the target posting's specific terminology.
2. Markdown Preservation +
Requesting Markdown formatting ensures bullet hierarchies and bold headers remain intact when pasted into word processors.
3. Burst Management +
Staying under the 15 RPM threshold prevents interruptions during intensive iterative prompt tuning sessions.
