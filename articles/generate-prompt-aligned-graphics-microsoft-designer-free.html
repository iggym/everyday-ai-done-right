Generate Custom Reference Images and Graphics for Free

Generate high-quality, prompt-aligned images for presentations or art references using Microsoft Designer’s free tier—no credit card required, but limited to 15 fast "boosts" per day before switching to a slower, unlimited queue.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Access the Tool

Go to Microsoft Designer Image Creator and sign in with a free Microsoft account. No payment method is requested.

Step 2: Craft Your Prompt

Describe your image with specific details. Use the templates below to get precise results on the first try.

A highly detailed, photorealistic reference photo of a [subject] in [lighting condition], neutral background, 85mm lens style.
A clean, modern flat vector icon of [concept], using a [color] color palette, minimalist design, white background.

Step 3: Generate and Download

Click "Create". Each generation uses 1 boost (you get 15 per day). Once depleted, generations continue at a slower speed, completely free and unlimited. Download your selected image as a PNG.

⚠️ What Breaks (Limitations)

  • Speed Drop: After 15 boosts, generation can take 1–3 minutes per image depending on server load.
  • Watermarks: Microsoft may append a small, subtle AI-generated watermark to free-tier downloads.
  • Content Filters: Strict safety filters will block prompts requesting copyrighted characters, realistic public figures, or restricted content.
  1. Account Status: Ensure you are logged into a standard personal Microsoft account, not an enterprise account with restricted AI policies.
  2. Boost Reset Time: Boosts reset on a rolling 24-hour clock from when you used them, not at midnight.
  3. Aspect Ratio: The free web interface defaults to 1:1 (square). For other ratios, you may need to crop afterward or use the broader Microsoft Designer app.
