Turn Dense PDFs Into Structured Study Notes

Upload a 100-page textbook PDF into Google AI Studio's free tier and generate structured, page-referenced study notes and flashcards—no credit card required, up to 1,500 requests a day.

Step 1 of 5
Read the uploaded PDF. Generate comprehensive study notes grouped by major topic. For every key concept, provide a 2-sentence summary. At the end, generate a code block containing flashcards in CSV format (Front, Back). Append the page number [p. X] to the end of every summary and flashcard answer to ground the information in the source text. Copied!
What breaks:
The free tier limits you to 15 requests per minute. If you spam the "Run" button, you will hit a 429 rate limit error and must wait 60 seconds. You also have 1,500 requests per day. If you exceed this, you are locked out until midnight Pacific Time. Do not upload highly sensitive documents as data may be used to improve services.
  • Bypass web app limits: Hosted chat apps cap uploads, but AI Studio allows processing massive textbooks (up to 1M tokens) for free.
  • CSV output for Anki: Formatting flashcards as raw CSV allows direct import into open-source study apps like Anki.
  • Page-referenced citations: Forcing the AI to append [p. X] grounds the output in the source text, preventing hallucinated study material.
