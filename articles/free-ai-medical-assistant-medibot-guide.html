Step-by-Step Walkthrough

1 Stop trusting apps that promise diagnosis.

Contrarian Correction: Most guides tell you to find an AI that "diagnoses" symptoms. That's backwards. The first job of a health assistant is to detect emergencies and escalate to real care — not to play doctor.

MediBot's red-flag triage detects cardiac, neurological, respiratory, obstetric, pediatric, and mental-health emergencies in every supported language — and immediately gives you the local emergency number for 190+ countries.[citation:4][citation:8]

So What? An app that doesn't escalate emergencies could delay real care. MediBot's first check is "is this an emergency?"

2 Open MediBot in your browser.

No installation required. It's a Progressive Web App (PWA) — you can add it to your phone's home screen and use it like a native app.[citation:4][citation:8]

  • Go to the Hugging Face Space or GitHub Pages deployment.
  • No sign-up. No API key. No paywall. No ads.[citation:4][citation:8]
3 Type or speak in your language.

MediBot auto-detects from 20 languages including English, Spanish, French, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, and Russian.[citation:4][citation:8]

It adapts to your region — °C/°F, metric/imperial, local guidance.

4 Understand the structured response.

Every answer follows a structured format:[citation:4][citation:8]

  • Summary — what the issue likely is
  • Possible causes — based on WHO/CDC/NHS/NIH/ICD-11/BNF/EMA guidelines
  • Self-care — what you can try at home
  • When to see a doctor — clear escalation thresholds

So What? This isn't a generic LLM response — it's grounded in authoritative medical sources.[citation:4][citation:8]

5 Share and stay private.

Every AI response has a Share button that generates a clean deep link with a branded preview — perfect for WhatsApp, Twitter, or Telegram.[citation:4]

Privacy matters: Zero accounts. Zero server-side conversation storage. No IPs logged. Anonymous session counter only.[citation:4][citation:8]

⚠️ What Breaks (Limitations)

  • It's not a doctor. The system explicitly states: "This system is for assistance only; it is not medical advice and does not replace professional care." So what? Trusting any AI for diagnosis could be harmful — MediBot is designed to escalate, not replace.
  • Hosted, not self-hosted. Privacy is anonymous, but your queries do leave your device.[citation:4] If you need 100% local, consider self-hosted alternatives like HiMe[citation:1] or HealthClaw.[citation:9]
  • Internet required. Unlike self-hosted options, MediBot requires a connection to the Hugging Face Inference API.[citation:4][citation:8]
