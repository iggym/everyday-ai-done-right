Turn Pantry Photos Into Weekly Meal Plans for Free

Snap a photo of your fridge or pantry with Google Gemini's free tier to generate a week of customized meal plans and a consolidated grocery list—no credit card required, though subject to a dynamic daily prompt limit.

Step 1: Access the Free Interface

Navigate to gemini.google.com and sign in with your standard Google account. No payment information or trial enrollment is required.

Step 2: Prepare Your Visual Input

Take a clear, well-lit photo of your open refrigerator, pantry shelves, or a collection of ingredients on your counter. Ensure text on labels is readable.

Step 3: Apply the Prompt Template

Upload the photo to the chat interface and paste the following template, filling in your specific dietary needs:

Act as a professional meal planner. I am providing a photo of my current pantry and refrigerator. 1. Identify all visible ingredients. 2. Propose 3 distinct dinner recipes using primarily these ingredients, assuming I have basic staples (salt, pepper, oil, flour). 3. Generate a consolidated grocery list for any missing ingredients needed for these 3 recipes. 4. Format the output as: - Identified Ingredients - Recipe 1, 2, 3 (with brief instructions) - Grocery List (categorized by produce, dairy, etc.) My dietary restrictions are: [insert restrictions, e.g., none, vegetarian, gluten-free].

Step 4: Refine and Export

Review the proposed recipes. If an ingredient is missing or unappealing, reply with "Swap recipe 2 for something using [alternative ingredient]". Once satisfied, copy the grocery list directly into your phone's notes app.

What Breaks (Limitations)

Google applies a dynamic daily prompt limit to the free tier that can fluctuate based on server load [[5]]. Heavy batch processing may hit this cap. Additionally, optical character recognition (OCR) may misread handwritten labels or poorly lit packaging, requiring manual correction in your follow-up prompts.

Three Things to Know

1. Dynamic Reset Window +
Unlike fixed midnight resets, dynamic limits often roll over based on your initial usage time or a 24-hour sliding window. Monitor the interface for limit warnings.
2. Staple Assumptions +
The prompt assumes you have basic staples (oil, salt, spices). If you are starting from absolute zero, explicitly state this in the dietary restrictions line to avoid recipes requiring ingredients you don't have.
3. Privacy Best Practices +
Avoid capturing sensitive mail, prescription bottles, or personal documents in the background of your pantry photos. Crop the image to focus solely on the food items.
