The Walkthrough

Demand the Real Bill

Call the hospital billing department. Do not accept the "summary" bill. You have a legal right to an Itemized Statement with CPT/HCPCS codes. Request this as a PDF.

"I am requesting a complete Itemized Statement including all CPT codes and revenue codes for my visit on [Date]. Please email it as a PDF."

The OCR Pre-Flight Check

Open the PDF on your computer. Try to highlight the text. If you cannot highlight the numbers (it acts like a single image), the AI will hallucinate the math. You must first run it through your phone's built-in Live Text (iOS) or Microsoft Lens (Android) to convert it to a real, selectable PDF.

Upload to DeepSeek

Deploy the Auditor Prompt

Deploy the Charity Care Shield

If the bill is still too high, ask the AI to invoke federal law to pause collections:

Three Things to Know

The "Itemized Statement" Legal Right

Hospitals hide junk fees in summary bills. You have a legal right to demand a line-by-line bill with CPT codes. Just asking for this often causes the billing department to quietly drop "facility fees" before the AI even sees the document.

The OCR Pre-Flight Check

AI models cannot "see" math inside a flat JPEG. If you cannot highlight the text with your mouse, the AI is just guessing the pixels. Use your phone's camera to scan it into a real PDF before uploading.

The 501(r) Charity Care Lever

Under the ACA, nonprofit hospitals are legally required to forgive debt for low/middle-income patients (often up to 400% of the poverty line). They will not offer it to you unless you formally cite IRS Section 501(r) and ask for the FAP application.
