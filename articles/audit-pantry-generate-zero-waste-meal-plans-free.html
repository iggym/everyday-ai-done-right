Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Extract Inventory

Snap a photo of your fridge interior or paste raw text from your latest store receipt.

Step 2: Run the Zero-Waste Prompt

Copy the prompt template below into your preferred free LLM interface:

You are an expert zero-waste household chef and kitchen manager. 
Analyze the following list of pantry/fridge ingredients:
[PASTE YOUR INGREDIENT LIST OR RECEIPT HERE]

Tasks:
1. Identify high-perishability items that must be used within 3 days.
2. Generate 3 complete meal recipes (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) using ONLY available ingredients plus standard spice rack staples.
3. Output a minimal 'Missing Gap' list of under $10 total cost to unlock 2 additional meals.
4. Format output strictly as Markdown tables.

Step 3: Cook & Save

Save the generated Markdown table directly into your digital household planner.

What Breaks: Computer vision can misinterpret items without clear front-of-package labels or handwritten store receipts. Expiration dates cannot be inferred unless explicitly visible on the image.
