⚠️ What Breaks

Claude's free tier enforces a rolling message quota (approximately 20 to 40 messages per 5-hour window). If you paste excessively large documents or iterate back and forth too rapidly across multiple job applications within a single afternoon, your session pauses until the rolling window clears.

Three Things to Know

1. The Rolling Window Trap +
The free tier tracks usage over a rolling 5-hour window, not a daily reset. If you burn your quota at 2:00 PM, access returns gradually around 7:00 PM, not midnight.
2. Column Layout Blindness +
ATS parsers read left-to-right across page sections. Multi-column resume templates cause parsers to interleave unrelated sentences into gibberish before the AI evaluates them.
3. Hallucination Guardrails +
Asking an AI to "improve" a resume often encourages fake credentials. Restricting the prompt strictly to your actual input text prevents dangerous resume inflation.
