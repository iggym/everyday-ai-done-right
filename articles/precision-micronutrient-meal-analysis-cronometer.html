Step-by-Step Workflow

Step 1: Set NCCDB as Primary Database

Create a free account on Cronometer. Under Settings -> Targets, enable the NCCDB database. This ensures every entry pulls from laboratory-analyzed food samples covering 84 vitamins, minerals, and fatty acids.

Step 2: Create Custom Recipe with Raw Weights

Weigh all raw ingredients on a digital scale before cooking. Click Foods -> Create Custom Recipe and input exact raw gram quantities to capture unheated nutrient profiles.

Step 3: Measure Total Cooked Yield Weight

Weigh the entire cooked dish after preparation. Enter the weight into the Recipe Yield field (e.g., 1200g). When eating, weigh your individual portion (e.g., 200g) for automatic moisture-adjusted nutrient distribution.
