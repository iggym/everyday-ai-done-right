The Free AI Workflow

Commercial receipt and document scanning apps regularly charge $5–$10/month for basic OCR and calendar extraction features. By leveraging Google AI Studio's vision processing on Gemini 2.0 Flash, you can process high-resolution photos of physical mail and generate standardized .ics calendar files or CSV tables completely free of charge.

Step-by-Step Execution

  1. Capture your paper notice: Take a clear photo of the document (jury summons, tax bill, utility shutoff notice, or school permission slip) on your phone or desktop.
  2. Access Google AI Studio: Navigate to aistudio.google.com and log in with any standard Google account. Select the Gemini 2.0 Flash model from the right panel.
  3. Upload image and paste prompt: Click the + button in the prompt box to attach your photo. Copy and paste the system extraction prompt below into the text field, then click Run.
  4. Export to Calendar: Copy the generated BEGIN:VCALENDAR... code block, paste it into a blank text document, save it as event.ics, and open it to automatically add the reminder to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook.
