Step-by-Step Walkthrough

1 Stop. AI is not your doctor.

Contrarian Correction: Most guides tell you to trust AI health apps for medical advice. That's backwards. Even apps with "therapist" in the name explicitly warn: "This is not a substitute for professional care." [citation:2][citation:3][citation:7]

So What? Relying on AI for medical advice could delay real care. Use AI for tracking, not diagnosis.

2 Install FitBuddy from F-Droid

FitBuddy is a free, open-source health tracker for Android [citation:4].

  • Android only: Available on F-Droid (free, no Google Play required).
  • No account required: Install and open.
  • No ads, no tracking by default [citation:4].
f-droid.org/packages/com.anant.fitbuddy/
3 Choose your AI provider (optional)

FitBuddy can run entirely offline using its built-in simulator [citation:4]. For photo logging, configure an AI provider:

  • Ollama (Local): Install Ollama, pull a vision model (e.g., LLaVA), point FitBuddy to http://localhost:11434 — 100% free, fully local.
  • Google Gemini: Free API key from Google AI Studio.
  • OpenRouter: Free vision models with automatic model dropdown.
4 Log your first meal

Snap a photo of your food or type what you ate. The AI estimates calories and macros instantly. Review ingredients, adjust weights, and save [citation:4].

So What? Logging just one meal breaks the inertia. Once you see the macro breakdown, you'll understand your eating patterns.

5 Bookmark presets and check your dashboard

For meals you eat regularly, save them as presets. Next time, it's one tap to log [citation:4].

Your dashboard shows: daily calorie ring, combined food/exercise log, macro breakdown, and custom charts tracking trends over time [citation:4].

⚠️ What Breaks (Limitations)

  • It's not a doctor. The app is a tracker, not a diagnostician. So what? Trusting it to diagnose you could be harmful.
  • Photo logging requires an AI key. Without configuring an AI provider, you can still log manually or use the simulator [citation:4].
  • Android only. No iOS version at this time.
  • Beta features. AI chatbot and multi-user support are in beta and may have bugs [citation:4].
