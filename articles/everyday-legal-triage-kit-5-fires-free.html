What Breaks — coral callout

  • Not legal advice. This kit organizes public law for research; LII and agencies provide info, not counsel.
  • 50-state variance: eviction notice periods (3 vs 30 days), wage claim lookback (2 vs 3 years), non-compete bans — always check your state via LII State Law Links.
  • Deadlines matter most: housing and benefits denials often have 10-30 day appeal windows — file first, perfect later.
  • Privacy: redact SSN, DOB, full account numbers before OCR/upload to any AI. Keep evidence pack local.
  • CFPB DB lag ~15 days; HUD counselors free but busy — book early.
