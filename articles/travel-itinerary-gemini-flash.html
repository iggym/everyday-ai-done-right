4 Full Guide — Travel Itinerary with Gemini Flash

Pitch: Build a day‑by‑day travel plan for any city, with real‑time landmarks, weather expectations, and cost estimates — 100% free, 60 requests per minute, no card.

Replaced costs: Instead of paying $20/mo for ChatGPT Plus or $15 for a travel planning app, use Gemini Flash in Google AI Studio. The free tier gives you 1,000 requests/day — enough for 5–10 detailed itineraries.

Step‑by‑Step Walkthrough

1
Open Google AI Studio
Go to aistudio.google.com and sign in with any Google account (no credit card).
2
Select “Gemini Flash” model
In the model dropdown, choose Gemini 1.5 Flash – it’s fast, free, and handles structured output.
3
Copy & paste the itinerary prompt
Plan a 5‑day trip to [city] for [budget] with 2 adults. Include daily must‑see landmarks, estimated meal costs, and a 3‑sentence weather note for each day. Use bullet points.
4
Replace [city] & [budget] & run
Enter your destination and total budget (e.g. “Barcelona” and “€1200”). Click “Run” to generate the full itinerary.
⚠️ What Breaks (Limitations)
  • 1,000 requests/day – if you run many iterations, spread across days.
  • No persistent memory – each chat is fresh; save good outputs manually.
  • Weather data is model‑generated (not live) – cross‑check with a weather app.
  • Rate limit: 60 requests/min – fine for manual use, but avoid scripted bursts.
