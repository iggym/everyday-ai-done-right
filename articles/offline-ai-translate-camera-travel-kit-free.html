What Breaks — Limitations

  • Language coverage: Apple offline ~19 languages (iOS 17 list includes EN, FR, DE, JA, KO, ES, IT, PT-BR, RU, AR, etc. — check your pair). Google offline ~59 languages for camera. If you need Khmer, Lao, Amharic offline, Google doesn't have camera, Apple doesn't have language at all.
  • Quality drop offline: On-device NMT is smaller model than cloud. Idioms, slang, long sentences degrade 10-20%. Keep input short: 1 sentence = better.
  • Camera needs conditions: Low light, cursive handwriting, stylized fonts fail OCR. Hold 6-10 inches away, steady 2 sec.
  • No real-time sign reading while moving: Conversation mode needs pause between speakers. Live overlay not persistent video translation.
  • Storage: Each pack ~40-200 MB. Downloading 8 languages can use 1+ GB. Pre-download on Wi-Fi.
  • Optional Gemini phrasebook: Free tier is 250 RPD for Flash (10 RPM) / 1000 RPD for Flash-Lite (15 RPM), 250K TPM, per official rate limits. Resets midnight PT. No card, but prompts may be used to improve Google products — don't paste passport numbers.
