⚠️ What Breaks (Limitations)

Most guides tell you to use NotebookLM as a passive summarizer for your existing drafts. That is backwards and wastes its unique "grounded" architecture.

  • The Sycophancy Trap: If you only upload your own notes, the model's alignment training pushes it to validate your ideas, creating an echo chamber. The external PDF is mandatory to bypass this.
  • Image-Based PDFs: NotebookLM cannot process scanned documents without prior OCR. If your external source is a scan, run it through a free OCR tool first, or the AI will read it as blank.
  • Audio Overview Quota Burn: The audio overview feature will attempt to generate "podcast" banter about your lore. This is useless for stress-testing and will consume your daily generation quota. Disable it.
