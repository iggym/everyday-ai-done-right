Step 1 of 4

1. Get the official anchor

Go to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (consumerfinance.gov) and download their free "Debt Collection" guide or sample validation letter PDF.

2. Start a free DeepSeek session

Go to chat.deepseek.com and sign up with an email. No credit card is required.

3. Upload both documents & run the prompt

Use the attachment icon to upload the CFPB PDF and a photo/PDF of the scary letter you received. Then paste this:

Act as a consumer rights researcher. Review the attached debt collection letter against the attached CFPB guidelines. 

Do not give legal advice. Instead, provide a plain-language summary of what this letter is actually asking for. Then, scan the letter for potential violations of the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA)—such as failing to provide a 30-day validation notice, threatening illegal actions, or misrepresenting the debt amount. 

Finally, draft a formal "Debt Validation Request" template I can mail to them, based on the CFPB's standard script, with placeholders for my specific information.

4. Review and mail

Copy the generated template into a word processor, fill in your details, and send it via Certified Mail with Return Receipt Requested. This forces the collector to legally verify the debt before continuing collection efforts.

What breaks

  • Dynamic Rate Limits: DeepSeek does not publish a hard daily message cap. During peak hours, you might hit a "Server busy" error. If this happens, wait 1–2 hours for the dynamic load to balance.
  • Not Legal Advice: The AI can spot obvious FDCPA violations, but it cannot replace a lawyer if you are actually being sued or facing wage garnishment.
  • Ladder Tier: T0 (AI) + T4 (Government Data).

Three non-obvious things this teaches

1. The FDCPA Violation Scanner

Prompting the AI to specifically scan the letter for violations of the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (e.g., threatening jail time, failing to provide a 30-day validation notice) turns a scary letter into a potential leverage point for a countersuit or settlement.

2. The CFPB Script Anchor

By uploading the CFPB's official PDF alongside your letter, you force the AI to ground its analysis in federal consumer protection law rather than hallucinating generic advice or inventing fake legal statutes.

3. The Dynamic Rate Limit Workaround

Because DeepSeek's limits are dynamic and unpublished, the safest way to avoid getting cut off mid-analysis is to upload both documents and ask for the entire validation template in one massive initial prompt, rather than asking follow-up questions in the same thread.
