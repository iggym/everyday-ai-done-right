What Breaks & Honest Failure Modes
  • Rolling Quota Depletion: Sending individual messages for every single item will trigger rate limits during peak demand hours. Always consolidate into a single end-of-day prompt.
  • Estimation Limits: AI models calculate macros based on standard USDA averages. They cannot guess hidden cooking oils or exact restaurant portion weights.
  • Privacy Boundary: Transcripts on the free tier may be processed for safety monitoring. Omit explicit legal names or medical diagnosis numbers from your voice notes.

Three Things to Remember

Sending one consolidated transcript at night uses exactly 1 message out of your ~30 message 5-hour allowance, keeping your usage permanently inside the free tier.
LLMs hallucinate numbers when given ambiguous inputs. Requiring the prompt to explicitly label estimated portion sizes keeps your macro totals grounded in reality.
Forcing the AI to split the output into a pure Markdown table followed by a notes section allows clean copy-pasting directly into spreadsheet applications without prose clutter.
