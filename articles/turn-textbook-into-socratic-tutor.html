Turn Any Textbook Into a Strict Socratic Tutor

Mistral's Vibe (formerly Le Chat) gives you 25 high-quality messages per day for free, with no credit card required, and its PDF upload turns any open-source textbook into an interactive tutor that forces you to do the thinking.

The Walkthrough

Sign Up

Go to chat.mistral.ai and create an account with an email address. No payment method is required.

Source the Material

Download a free, public-domain textbook chapter from OpenStax (openstax.org) as a PDF.

Upload

Start a new chat in Vibe and use the attachment icon to upload the OpenStax PDF. Vibe will index the entire document.

Deploy the Prompt

Paste this exact instruction to lock the AI into teaching mode:

Act as a strict Socratic tutor. I am studying the attached document. Do not give me the answers or summarize the text. Ask me one guiding question at a time based on the core concepts. Wait for my response. If I am wrong, give me a subtle hint. If I am right, move to the next concept. Let's begin with the most foundational concept in this chapter.

Engage

Answer the AI's questions out loud or type them. If you want to build a study guide, click the "Canvas" icon to open a side panel where the AI can build a running comparison table of the concepts you're mastering.

⚠️ What Breaks

The free tier is hard-capped at roughly 25 messages per 24-hour rolling window. If you try to cram for a final exam in one 4-hour session, you will hit a paywall prompt asking you to upgrade to the $15/mo Pro plan. You must treat this as a "one concept per day" deep-work tool, not a bulk-cramming engine. It also requires the PDF to have selectable text (scanned image PDFs will fail unless you OCR them first).

Three Things to Know

The Socratic Prompt Trick

The 25-message daily limit is actually a pedagogical feature if you use the Socratic prompt. By forcing the AI to ask you questions one at a time, you get 12 deep, active-recall turns per day, which is proven to be superior for retention than burning 25 messages asking "What is X?"

The OpenStax PDF Hack

Instead of copy-pasting text (which wastes tokens and hallucinates facts), upload the free, public-domain OpenStax textbook directly into Vibe. It indexes the entire chapter, allowing you to ask "Quiz me on the section about mitochondrial DNA" with perfect accuracy.

The Canvas Mode for Cheat Sheets

Vibe's "Canvas" feature lets you collaborate on a living study guide. You can ask the AI to build a table of comparisons (e.g., "Mitosis vs. Meiosis") and edit it in real-time, turning the chat into a persistent, exportable cheat sheet.
