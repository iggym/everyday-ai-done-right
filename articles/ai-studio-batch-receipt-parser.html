Bulk Extract Receipts to CSV for Free

Stop doing manual data entry for your expense reports; drop up to 50 receipts at a time into Google's free developer sandbox and instantly get a perfectly formatted CSV.

Select the data points you need to extract. The sandbox will dynamically generate the exact System Instruction required to force the AI to return a clean CSV without conversational fluff.


            

        


        
        

            Stop typing out expense reports. You can drop 50 PDFs into Google AI Studio and get a perfect CSV in 14 seconds without writing a line of code.
            
        


        

            
I once spent an entire Sunday afternoon manually typing 80 crumpled conference receipts into a spreadsheet, only to realize later I had transcribed the tax lines incorrectly on half of them. A month later, I tried uploading them to a standard consumer AI chatbot. It hallucinated the formatting, grouped things wrong, and refused to process more than three files before complaining about file size. The mistake was using a consumer chat interface for bulk data extraction.

            
            
The correct tool is Google AI Studio. It is a developer playground, but you do not need to be a developer to use it. You upload your files, give it strict instructions, and it complies.


            

                
Free Tier Limits (Verified: Aug 20, 2026)

                
15 Requests Per Minute | 1,000,000 Tokens Per Minute | 1,500 Requests Per Day

                
These limits are high enough to process a small business's entire annual expense backlog in under an hour.

            


            
    
                
  1. Open Google AI Studio: Navigate to aistudio.google.com and sign in with a standard Google account.
    2. 
                
  2. Select the Right Model: On the right sidebar, ensure the model is set to Gemini 1.5 Flash. This model is heavily optimized for fast, multimodal document reading.
    3. 
                
  3. Set the System Instructions: Use the interactive schema sandbox above to generate your strict instructions, then paste them into the "System Instructions" box on the left side of AI Studio.
    4. 
                
  4. Batch Upload: Click the "+" button next to the prompt box. Select up to 50 PDFs or images of your receipts.
    5. 
                
  5. Run the Extraction: In the text box, simply type "Extract all data to CSV." Hit run.
    6. 
            

            
            
The output will be a raw CSV code block. Copy it, open your spreadsheet software (Excel/Google Sheets), and paste.

        


        

            
What Breaks

            
    
                
  • Handwriting degradation: If the receipt is heavily crumpled or written in faint pencil, the model may confidently guess numbers rather than admit it cannot read them. Always spot-check the largest expenses.
    • 
                
  • Privacy considerations: AI Studio is a developer tool. Google states they may use data submitted through the free tier to improve their products. Do not use this workflow for receipts containing HIPAA-protected medical data or highly classified legal expenses.
