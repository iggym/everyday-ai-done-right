HOME HEALTH BINDER — INTAKE PROMPT (use in Sheets/Notebook)

Paste OCR text from lab PDF below, then for each test:
1. Go to https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/ → search test name
2. Copy: What is it, Why ordered, Normal result meaning
3. In Sheet: Columns = Test | Your Result | Reference Range | MedlinePlus Plain English | Question for Doctor
4. Use formula: =IF(VALUE > High, "↑ Ask doctor", IF(VALUE < Low, "↓ Ask doctor", "✓"))

Constraints: Do NOT interpret. Only organize official info + your result for clinician visit.
DRUG CHECK — STEP-BY-STEP
1. Photo each pill bottle with Google Lens → Copy text: Name, Dose, Rx #
2. Go to drugs.com/drug-interactions
3. Add Drug 1, Drug 2, Drug 3 → Check (no account needed)
4. For 4+ drugs: Create free account (email only, no card) → saves list
5. Export PDF → add to Binder tab "Interactions"
6. Rule: Always check + food + alcohol + supplements — Drugs.com includes those

Add to Sheet: Medication | Dose | Purpose (from MedlinePlus) | Interaction Flag | Doctor Question

What Breaks — coral callout

  • Not medical advice. This binder organizes information; decisions stay with you + clinician. MedlinePlus explicitly says "written for general public" not diagnosis【6581865443986613499†L5-L9】.
  • Drugs.com free tier: viewing report with 4+ meds requires free sign-in【8749461057462939650†L51-L54】. Create account with email only — no card, still T4 public benefit.
  • OCR errors: Lens can misread "mg" as "mg" or "10" as "1O" — always verify against bottle.
  • Lab ranges vary by lab — MedlinePlus gives general info, your lab's reference range on report is authoritative.
  • Privacy: Keep Binder in private Google Drive or local Sheet; do not upload identifiable labs to public AI chatbots.
