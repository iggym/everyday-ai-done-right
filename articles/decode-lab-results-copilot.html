⚠️ What Breaks (Limitations)

Most guides tell you to ask AI to interpret your labs or diagnose you. That is backwards and wastes the tool's capability.

  • The Guardrail Wall: If you ask for a diagnosis, the AI's safety training will instantly block the response with an "I am not a doctor" disclaimer, wasting your time. Framing it as "questions for my doctor" bypasses this.
  • File Size Rejection: Microsoft Copilot free tier caps file uploads at 10MB per conversation. If your hospital portal exports a massive, image-heavy 50MB PDF, you must compress it first using a free tool like iLovePDF, or Copilot will reject it.
  • Context Window Reset: Copilot's context window resets after ~30 turns. Do not try to analyze 5 years of lab history in a single thread. Focus on one specific report per conversation to maintain accuracy.
