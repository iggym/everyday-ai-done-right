Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Step 1: Classify the Dispute

Identify whether your issue involves debt collection (FDCPA), tenant deposits, unfair billing, or warranty non-compliance.

Step 2: Run the Triage Prompt

Copy and paste your dispute details into any free AI interface with this prompt:

You are an expert consumer advocacy and legal self-triage assistant.
Analyze the following everyday consumer dispute:
[PASTE DETAILS, LEASE TERMS, BILL, OR TEXT HISTORY HERE]

Tasks:
1. Identify the core everyday legal category (e.g., FDCPA violation, improper security deposit withholding, deceptive billing).
2. Cite the primary federal or state statutory protections applying to this scenario.
3. Draft a formal Dispute & Cease-and-Desist Notice requiring resolution within 10 business days.
4. Provide a structured checklist of official federal/state regulatory agencies (CFPB, FTC, State AG) where a complaint can be filed if non-compliant.

Step 3: Escalate to Federal Web Portals

If the opposing party fails to resolve the issue within 10 days, submit your generated notice directly to ConsumerFinance.gov or FTC.gov.

What Breaks: Generative prompts do not constitute attorney-client privilege and cannot file official court motions or represent you in formal legal trials.
