🔎 Three Things Most Guides Don’t Tell You

1. The “free” tier resets on a rolling window — not midnight ▼
Gemini’s 60 req/min is a sliding window. If you hit it, wait 60 seconds from the first request, not until the next hour. Most people misunderstand this and think they’re blocked for hours.
2. You can use Gemini + ChatGPT simultaneously for free ▼
They have separate quotas. If one is rate-limited, switch to the other. This effectively doubles your free capacity without paying.
3. Data retention: Gemini keeps your prompts for 30 days by default ▼
If you handle sensitive data, use the “Do not use my data to improve models” toggle in Google AI Studio settings. ChatGPT also has a data control setting.
