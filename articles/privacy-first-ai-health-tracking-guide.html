Step-by-Step Walkthrough

1 Stop trusting cloud-hosted health apps.

Contrarian Correction: Most guides tell you to download a popular tracker and let it analyze your health. That's backwards. These apps are data businesses first. The real win is using a bring-your-own-key (BYOK) app.

So What? If you use a closed-source tracker, your meal logs, weight, and health patterns are a product. With Fud AI or FitBuddy, they're yours. No account, no cloud sync by default.

2 Install the right app for your device.
  • iOS: Install Fud AI from the App Store. Open source, MIT licensed, no ads, no account [citation:5].
  • Android: Install FitBuddy from F-Droid. Open source, GPL-3.0, no ads, no tracking by default [citation:2].
3 Get a free Gemini API key.

Both apps use the same free tier:

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com
  2. Sign in with your Google account.
  3. Click "Get API Key" — it's free.
  4. Copy the key.

So What? Without this key, you can still log meals manually, but photo recognition requires it [citation:5]. The free Gemini tier gives you enough quota for daily tracking.

aistudio.google.com → Get API Key

Optional: Configure Ollama if you run a local LLM [citation:2].

4 Configure the app and log your first meal.
  • Open Settings → paste your Gemini API key.
  • Snap a photo of your food (Fud AI supports up to 10 images at once [citation:5]).
  • The AI estimates calories and macros instantly.
  • Review ingredients, adjust weights, and save.
  • Bookmark favorite meals as presets for one-tap logging next time [citation:2].
5 Track more than food.
  • Fud AI: 873 exercises with photos, barcode scanning, Apple Watch complications, water tracking, Siri shortcuts [citation:5].
  • FitBuddy: workouts, barcode scan, AES-256-GCM encrypted backups [citation:8].

Check your dashboard: daily calorie ring, combined food/exercise log, macro breakdown, custom charts tracking trends over time [citation:2].

⚠️ What Breaks (Limitations)

  • It's not a doctor. Both apps are trackers, not diagnosticians. So what? Trusting them to diagnose you could be harmful.
  • Bring-your-own-key required for photo logging. Free Gemini key takes 1 minute to create. So what? This is the privacy tradeoff — your data isn't stored on their servers, but you need to bring the AI.
  • Fud AI is iOS-only. Android users should use FitBuddy [citation:2].
  • FitBuddy is Android-only. iOS users should use Fud AI [citation:5].
  • No cross-device sync unless you use optional cloud backup (off by default in FitBuddy) [citation:8].
