Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Set Up Your Base Map

Open Google Maps in your browser or mobile app, search for your destination city, and zoom to an appropriate level for your planned activities.

Create a Custom List for Your Trip

Click "Saved" > "New list", name it "[Destination] [Date] Trip", and set visibility to "Private" (default). This list will hold all your planned stops.

Use AI-Powered Discovery for Attractions

Search for generic terms like "museums," "parks," or "restaurants" in your destination. Use the "Explore" tab to see AI-curated recommendations based on popularity and ratings.

[activity type] in [neighborhood/area of city] Example: "art galleries in Marais district Paris"

Build Your Itinerary with Smart Routing

Add selected places to your custom list, select multiple locations from your list, and click "Directions" to see AI-optimized routing. The system automatically calculates efficient travel order and times.

Leverage Immersive View for Planning

For major landmarks, look for the "Immersive View" option. This AI-generated 3D view helps you preview the area around attractions, see what it looks like at different times of day, and identify nearby amenities.

Use AI Summaries for Quick Decisions

Click on any place to see AI-generated summaries of popular times and wait estimates, key features and highlights, and common visitor experiences.

Adjust Plans with Real-Time AI Insights

While exploring, use Google Maps' real-time suggestions for alternative routes if areas are crowded, nearby alternatives if a planned stop is closed, and spontaneous additions based on your current location.

What Breaks (Limitations)

  • No multi-day timeline view—only single-day routing
  • Cannot automatically optimize for opening hours across multiple days
  • Limited to Google's data (some smaller venues may be missing)
  • No collaborative planning features without a Google account
  • Offline functionality requires manual map downloads and loses AI features

Three Things to Know

  1. Google Maps' AI features work entirely in your browser or app—nothing is installed on your device.
  2. The routing algorithm considers real-time traffic data, making it more accurate than static itinerary planners.
  3. Immersive View uses neural rendering to create 3D scenes that blend Street View and aerial imagery.

Try It Now

  2. Search for a city you'd like to visit

  4. Add 3-5 attractions using the discovery features
