Describe Any Photo and Ask Follow-Up Questions for Free with Be My AI

Snap a photo of anything with the free Be My Eyes app and Be My AI describes it aloud and answers follow-up questions — no credit card, no subscription, fully screen-reader optimized. It needs an internet connection and can misread details, so don't rely on it alone for safety-critical decisions.

01 What it does

For blind and low-vision users — and anyone helping someone who is — Be My AI turns a phone or Windows PC into a conversational visual assistant. Open the free Be My Eyes app, snap a photo, and Be My AI (powered by OpenAI's GPT-4 vision) describes what's in the frame and answers follow-up questions: "What's the expiration date on this milk?", "Is this a stop sign or a yield sign?", "Read me the top headline." It works on iOS, Android, and Windows, costs nothing, asks for no card, and is fully optimized for screen readers like VoiceOver and TalkBack.

05 Step-by-step walkthrough

06 Copyable follow-up prompts

After Be My AI describes your photo, type or paste any of these into the chat to go deeper.

Read me every line of text on this page, top to bottom, exactly as written.
What is the expiration date, and how much does this cost?
Describe the layout of this room and tell me where the doors and furniture are.
Is the indicator light on or off, and what color is it?
Summarize this letter in three sentences and tell me if anything needs a response by a date.

⚠ What breaks (limitations)

  • Needs internet. No offline use — descriptions are generated in the cloud.
  • Can misread details. Small text, low-contrast labels, handwriting, or unusual layouts may be read wrong or invented. Verify anything important.
  • Not safety-critical on its own. For medication dosage, street crossings, or steps, double-check — or call Be My Eyes' free human volunteers instead.
  • No wearables. Be My AI is not supported on smart glasses or wearable devices.
  • Long documents may be summarized rather than read verbatim; ask for exact text line-by-line if you need every word.
  • Photos auto-delete in 30 days if you don't save or share them — a privacy plus, but save anything you need to keep.
