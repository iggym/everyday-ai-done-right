Step 1: Account Creation

Navigate to Leonardo.ai and sign up using an email address or Google account. You will not be prompted for payment information.

Step 2: Access the Generation Canvas

From the left-hand dashboard, click "AI Image Generation" to open the canvas interface.

Step 3: Apply the Prompt Template

Copy and paste the following template into the prompt box, replacing the bracketed sections:

Character reference sheet, [subject description, e.g., a cyberpunk mechanic with neon goggles], multiple angles, front view, side view, back view, consistent lighting, neutral gray background, high detail, concept art style --no watermark, text, blurry

Step 4: Configure & Generate

Set Model to "Leonardo Phoenix" or "Vision XL", Dimensions to 1024x1024, and Number of Images to 4. Click "Generate". Use "Image Guidance" in subsequent prompts to maintain visual consistency.

What Breaks (Limitations)

The 150-token ceiling depletes after 30–50 generations depending on resolution. The reset occurs on a rolling 24-hour window from the time of use, not at midnight. Free-tier outputs may carry subtle platform branding depending on the selected model.

Three Things to Know

1. Rolling Reset Window +
Tokens reset exactly 24 hours after you spend them, not at a fixed daily midnight cutoff. Plan heavy generation sessions accordingly.
2. Commercial Use Rights +
Always verify the specific license of the model you select on the free tier. Some community models may have non-commercial restrictions.
3. Consistency is Manual +
AI does not inherently "remember" characters. You must actively use the "Image Guidance" or "Character Reference" upload features to lock in visual traits across generations.
