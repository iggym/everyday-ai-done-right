Draft a Community Newsletter with a Free OpenRouter Model

Write the monthly newsletter for a club, PTA, or small shop from a list of updates, using a free model variant and a reusable prompt.

Why this works

Volunteer organisations publish a newsletter, and it takes an evening each month. A free model can turn a bullet list of updates into a readable draft, which you then edit for the community.

Step by step

  1. Collect the updatesAsk committee members to send three bullet points each by the same deadline.
  2. Pick a free modelUse a model ID ending in :free from the OpenRouter models page.
  3. Generate the draftUse the prompt below. Ask for a subject line and two alternatives.
  4. Edit for names and datesCheck every name, date, and figure against the original bullets.
  5. Save the promptKeep the prompt in a text file so next month's issue has the same structure.

Check your result

  • Every date and name matches the bullets
  • No fundraising or money figures were invented
  • The subject line works without the body

Pitfalls to avoid

  • Free models can change between sessions; keep your prompt stable so tone stays consistent.
  • Email lists may have data rules; send only to people who subscribed.
