  1. Visit gemini.google.com

  1. Click the "+" button or attachment icon in the chat interface
  2. Select your document (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, or image)
  3. Wait for the upload confirmation (files up to 20MB accepted)
3
Analyze with Targeted Prompts

Use these specialized prompts for different document types:

For Contract Analysis:

Contract Analysis Prompt
Analyze this contract and identify: 1. Key obligations for each party 2. Termination conditions and notice periods 3. Liability limitations 4. Any unusual or potentially problematic clauses 5. Missing standard protections that should be added Format your response as a structured summary with specific clause references.

For Financial Document Analysis:

Financial Analysis Prompt
Review this financial document and extract: 1. Key financial metrics (revenue, expenses, profit margins) 2. Year-over-year comparisons if applicable 3. Notable trends or anomalies 4. Potential red flags or areas of concern 5. Questions a business owner should ask their accountant about Present findings in a clear, structured format with specific data points.

For Business Plan Review:

Business Plan Review Prompt
Evaluate this business plan across these dimensions: 1. Market analysis completeness and accuracy 2. Financial projections reasonableness 3. Competitive positioning clarity 4. Operational feasibility 5. Risk assessment thoroughness Rate each dimension 1-5 and provide specific improvement suggestions for any area scoring below 4.

For Spreadsheet Data Analysis:

Spreadsheet Analysis Prompt
Analyze this spreadsheet data and: 1. Identify the key trends and patterns 2. Highlight any outliers or anomalies 3. Calculate relevant summary statistics 4. Suggest visualizations that would best represent this data 5. Draw 3-5 business-relevant conclusions Be specific with data points and avoid generic observations.
4
Follow-Up and Refine

What Breaks (Limitations)

  • Rate Limits: 15 requests per minute, 1 million tokens per minute
  • File Size: Maximum 20MB per document
  • Accuracy: May miss nuanced legal interpretations; always verify critical contract points with a professional
  • Complexity: Struggles with extremely complex financial instruments or highly specialized legal documents
  • Privacy: Documents are processed by Google; avoid uploading highly sensitive or confidential information
  • Context Window: For very long documents, may not maintain full context across the entire file

Three Things to Know

1. Gemini works best with structured documents +
While Gemini can handle various document types, it performs best with well-structured documents like standard contracts, formatted financial reports, and organized spreadsheets. Scanned documents or poorly formatted PDFs may yield less accurate results.
2. You can combine multiple documents in one analysis +
Upload multiple related documents (like a contract and its amendments) in a single conversation to get comparative analysis. This is particularly useful for version comparisons or understanding how different documents relate to each other.
3. The free tier is sufficient for most small business needs +
Most small businesses analyze fewer than 50 documents per month. At 15 requests per minute, you could theoretically analyze over 20,000 documents per day if needed. The free tier is designed to handle typical small business workloads without hitting limits.
