# Brainstorm and Shortlist Names for a Side Project

- **Tool:** OpenRouter :free model variants
- **Cost:** $0 on :free variants — listed by OpenRouter as free versions of models
- **Limits:** Free variants have their own rate limits and availability; check the model page
- **Source:** https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/model-variants/free
- **Verified:** 2026-10-08
- **Guide:** https://iggym.github.io/everyday-ai-done-right/articles/openrouter-free-naming-brainstorm.html

## Steps

1. **Describe the project in two sentences.** Say who it is for and what it does.
2. **Generate a long list.** Use the prompt below to produce forty options across four styles.
3. **Apply the checklist.** Remove names that are hard to spell, already used by a well-known product, or mean something unfortunate in another language.
4. **Search for conflicts.** Check app stores, the company register in your country, and domain availability for each finalist. Trademark checks need a professional for anything serious.
5. **Test aloud.** Say the final three names to a friend and ask which they remember.

## Prompt

```text
Suggest 40 names for this project, in four groups of ten: descriptive, invented, metaphorical, and short (one or two syllables). Each name should be easy to spell and say. Output a simple numbered list with no explanations.

PROJECT:
<two sentences>
```

## Check your result

- [ ] Final names pass a spelling and pronunciation test
- [ ] Each finalist was searched in app stores and company registers
- [ ] Trademark questions were referred to a professional

## Pitfalls

- Models often suggest names already in use; always search.
- Naming is a judgement call; a shortlist of three is enough to decide.
