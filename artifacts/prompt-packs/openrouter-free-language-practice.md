# Practise a New Language with a Free OpenRouter Chat Model

- **Tool:** OpenRouter :free model variants
- **Cost:** $0 on :free variants — listed by OpenRouter as free versions of models
- **Limits:** Free variants have their own rate limits and availability; check the model page
- **Source:** https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/model-variants/free
- **Verified:** 2026-10-08
- **Guide:** https://iggym.github.io/everyday-ai-done-right/articles/openrouter-free-language-practice.html

## Steps

1. **Choose a level and a topic.** For example: 'A3 level, ordering food and asking directions.'
2. **Set the role with a system prompt.** Use the prompt below so the model replies at your level and corrects only what matters.
3. **Practise for ten minutes.** Write in the target language and try to answer without translating in your head.
4. **Collect new words.** Ask for three new words from the conversation, with an example sentence each.
5. **Review tomorrow.** Start the next session by asking the model to quiz you on yesterday's words.

## Prompt

```text
You are a friendly conversation partner for a learner at {level} in {language}. Reply in {language} with one or two short sentences. After each reply, add a 'Correction' line in English only if there is a mistake that changes meaning. Keep a list of three new words at the end of each session.
```

## Check your result

- [ ] Replies are at your level
- [ ] Corrections change meaning, not style
- [ ] Vocabulary is recorded in your own notes

## Pitfalls

- Models can make grammar mistakes too; check any rule against a textbook or native speaker.
- Free variants can be slower at peak times; keep sessions short.

_The ':free' variant behaviour was read from OpenRouter's documentation on 8 Oct 2026. Free models change often; check the model's page for current limits._
