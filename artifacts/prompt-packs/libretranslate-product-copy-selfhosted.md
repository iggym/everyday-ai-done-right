# Translate Product Descriptions into Three Languages for Free

- **Tool:** LibreTranslate (self-hosted, AGPL-3.0)
- **Cost:** $0 to self-host — open-source under AGPL-3.0
- **Limits:** Your own server's capacity; no quota
- **Source:** https://github.com/LibreTranslate/LibreTranslate
- **Verified:** 2026-10-08
- **Guide:** https://iggym.github.io/everyday-ai-done-right/articles/libretranslate-product-copy-selfhosted.html

## Steps

1. **Write clear English copy.** Short sentences translate more reliably than long ones.
2. **Translate description by description.** Paste one product at a time into LibreTranslate, once per target language.
3. **Check product facts.** Verify sizes, materials, and care instructions against your supplier data.
4. **Get a native check.** Ask a native speaker or a translator to review the top products first.
5. **Keep a glossary.** Record your product names so they are translated the same way every time.

## Prompt

```text
(Use the LibreTranslate interface or API.) Translate this product description from English to {target}. Keep product names, sizes, materials, and care instructions exactly as written.

DESCRIPTION:
<paste>
```

## Check your result

- [ ] Sizes, materials and care instructions match your supplier data
- [ ] A native speaker reviewed the bestselling products
- [ ] Product names follow your glossary

## Pitfalls

- Machine translation can make product claims stronger or weaker than you intended; check them.
- Consumer-law claims in each market differ; check local rules before you publish.
