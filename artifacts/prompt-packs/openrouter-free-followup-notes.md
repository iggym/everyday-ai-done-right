# Draft Thank-You and Follow-Up Notes After Every Interview

- **Tool:** OpenRouter :free model variants
- **Cost:** $0 on :free variants — listed by OpenRouter as free versions of models
- **Limits:** Free variants have their own rate limits and availability; check the model page
- **Source:** https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/model-variants/free
- **Verified:** 2026-10-08
- **Guide:** https://iggym.github.io/everyday-ai-done-right/articles/openrouter-free-followup-notes.html

## Steps

1. **Note three things right away.** Write the interviewer's name, one topic they cared about, and one question you want to answer better.
2. **Generate a draft.** Use the prompt below with a model ID ending in :free.
3. **Check the facts.** Confirm names, job title, and the date of the conversation.
4. **Send within 24 hours.** Keep it to four or five sentences.
5. **Track it.** Log the date and what you sent, so you can follow up once more after a week.

## Prompt

```text
Write a follow-up email after a job interview. Keep it under 120 words. Thank the interviewer by name, mention the topic below in one sentence, add one useful point I want to add, and close politely without asking for an update. Do not invent details.

NOTES:
<paste>
```

## Check your result

- [ ] Names and job title are correct
- [ ] The email references a real topic from the interview
- [ ] It was sent within one day

## Pitfalls

- Do not paste other candidates' information or confidential company details into a model.
- Generic follow-ups are easy to spot; add one sentence in your own words.
