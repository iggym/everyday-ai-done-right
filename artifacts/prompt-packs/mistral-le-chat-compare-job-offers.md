## Steps

1. **Write each offer in the same units.** Annual gross, bonus as a percentage, employer pension contribution, paid holiday days, and weekly commute time.
2. **Add what matters to you.** Learning budget, flexibility, and team size can matter as much as pay.
3. **Ask for the comparison.** Use the prompt below with both offers pasted in.
4. **Check the numbers yourself.** Calculate the total compensation of each offer by hand, including the pension.
5. **Ask the employers.** Write down questions for each employer, such as bonus history and how pension matching works.

## Prompt

```text
Compare these two job offers in a table: base pay, bonus, pension, holiday, commute, and any other benefits. Then list the three biggest trade-offs and any items that are missing from one offer but included in the other. Do not recommend a choice; do not calculate tax.
```

## Check your result

- [ ] Both offers use the same units
- [ ] Your own total compensation was checked by hand
- [ ] Questions for each employer are written down

## Pitfalls

- Do not share another employer's confidential terms or your current employer's private data.
- Tax and pension rules differ by country; get advice from a qualified adviser for large decisions.
