- **Tool:** Mistral Le Chat (Free plan)
- **Cost:** Free plan — no payment needed to start
- **Limits:** Limited messages, web searches, coding sessions and document uploads; exact allowances are shown in your account
- **Source:** https://mistral.ai/pricing/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-08
- **Guide:** https://iggym.github.io/everyday-ai-done-right/articles/mistral-le-chat-party-planner.html

## Steps

1. **Set the total budget.** Decide the maximum you will spend, including decorations and a buffer of about ten percent.
2. **List the guests and dietary needs.** Count adults and children, and note allergies.
3. **Generate the plan.** Use the prompt below to get a menu, a shopping list, and a cost estimate per line.
4. **Replace estimates with real prices.** Check two or three local shops and update the sheet.
5. **Track as you go.** Log each purchase against the budget, and drop the least important line first if you run over.

## Prompt

```text
Plan a party for {count} guests with a total budget of {budget}. Include: a simple menu that accommodates these dietary needs: {diet}, a shopping list grouped by shop section with estimated quantities, a cost estimate per line, and a total that stays within budget. Mark estimates clearly.
```

## Check your result

- [ ] Dietary needs are covered by the menu
- [ ] The total is within your budget after real prices
- [ ] Each line is tracked as you spend

## Pitfalls

- Food safety, especially for buffets and hot holding, needs proper guidance from a food-safety authority.
- Estimates are rough; replace them with real prices before you commit.
